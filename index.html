Dissertation focus

CORE TOPIC

Are coaching qualifications necessary in sport?

Explore the role of formal coaching qualifications in developing coaching knowledge, athlete safety, effective practice and coach confidence. Keep the question open to evidence that supports and challenges the value of qualifications.

Suggested starting point · refine this focus with your supervisor.
